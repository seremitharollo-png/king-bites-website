Our story

King Bites Grill started as a single grill on a Johannesburg street corner, with one simple idea: great grilled food should not cost a fortune.

Every burger, chicken and wing is marinated in-house and cooked over real flame when you order. Our sauces are made in small batches, and our chips are cut fresh each morning.

We keep prices fair, portions generous and service friendly. Whether you are grabbing lunch between meetings or feeding the whole family, you will always be treated like royalty.
